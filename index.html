03What We Do

At Independent Pest Solutions, we offer a comprehensive range of pest control services designed to address virtually any pest problem you may encounter in the Puyallup area. Our services cover common household and commercial pests including ants, spiders, rodents, wasps, bed bugs, cockroaches, and much more. We take a thorough, systematic approach to pest management — starting with a detailed inspection of your property to identify the source of the problem before recommending and implementing the most effective treatment plan available.

What sets Independent Pest Solutions apart is our commitment to using proven, cutting-edge treatment methods that are both highly effective and safe for your family, pets, and the surrounding environment. We stay current with the latest advancements in pest control technology and techniques, ensuring that every treatment we provide meets the highest industry standards. Our technicians are fully licensed, trained, and equipped to handle pest challenges of any size or complexity with precision and professionalism.

We understand that every property is different, which is why we never take a one-size-fits-all approach to pest control. Instead, our team customizes each treatment plan based on the specific pest species, the severity of the infestation, and the unique layout and conditions of your home or business. This personalized strategy allows us to deliver faster, longer-lasting results while minimizing the disruption to your daily routine.

Independent Pest Solutions also offers ongoing maintenance programs designed to keep your property pest-free throughout every season. Regular preventative treatments are one of the most effective ways to stop infestations before they start, saving you time, stress, and money in the long run. Our flexible scheduling options make it easy to find a service plan that fits your lifestyle and budget, giving you consistent peace of mind all year long.

As a locally owned and operated business, Independent Pest Solutions is deeply invested in the health and well-being of the Puyallup community we serve.
